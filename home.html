BIG FLAVOR. LESS WAITING.

Welcome to Smoosh, a food truck concept built around one simple idea: great food should be worth the trip, not the wait.

We're bringing bold smashburgers, loaded hot dogs, crispy fries, and ice-cold drinks together with a convenient order-ahead experience.

Browse the menu, choose your favorites, and submit your order before heading to the truck. We'll give you an estimated pickup-ready time so you can spend less time waiting and more time enjoying your food.

MORE THAN JUST A BURGER

Smoosh started with a love for great food and the dream of building a food truck with its own personality.

We want to create an experience that's straightforward, satisfying, and a little different. Big flavors, memorable menu items, and food worth coming back for.

But great food is only half the experience. We also know that nobody wants to spend their lunch break standing in a long line.

That's why the Smoosh web application is designed to let customers explore the menu and build their orders ahead of time.

Our goal is simple: make getting great food as enjoyable as eating it.
